📘 Pure and Applied Mathematics

Making Mathematics Clear, Practical, and Accessible

Welcome to Pure and Applied Mathematics, an educational initiative founded by Momanyi Stephen to help college students and school learners understand mathematics through clear explanations, step-by-step solutions, and personalized online lessons.

Our goal is to make mathematics easier to understand, helping learners build confidence, strengthen their problem-solving skills, and achieve their academic goals.

🎯 Our Services

🎓 College Mathematics

We offer learning support in the following areas:

Linear Algebra — vectors, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, and eigenvectors.
Calculus I & II— limits, differentiation, integration, and applications.
Analytic Geometry — coordinate geometry, conic sections, and geometric analysis.
Basic Mathematics — essential mathematical concepts and techniques.
Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) — methods of solving differential equations and their applications.
Complex Analysis — complex numbers, complex functions, and analytical methods.

📚 High School and Remedial Mathematics

We also support high school learners and students who need additional help with foundational mathematics.

Our lessons focus on:

- Simplifying difficult mathematical concepts.
- Working through problems step by step.
- Strengthening foundational mathematical skills.
- Preparing for tests and examinations.
- Providing personalized learning support based on individual needs.
